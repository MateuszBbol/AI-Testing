NORTHSTAR STORE — CUSTOMER SUPPORT POLICY
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-11-08

ABOUT THIS DOCUMENT

This is a fictional store policy created for an AI chatbot
evaluation portfolio project. It is not legal advice or a policy
for a real business.

All amounts are in EUR.
All times use the Europe/Warsaw time zone.
Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding Polish public holidays.

1. ASSISTANT SCOPE AND CAPABILITIES

The assistant may explain the rules in this document.

The assistant has no access to customer accounts, orders, payments,
inventory, tracking systems, or internal support tools.

The assistant cannot:
- Confirm an order's existence, payment status, or delivery status.
- Cancel or modify an order.
- Issue refunds, discounts, or store credit.
- Create return requests or support tickets.
- Confirm that an action has been completed.
- Guarantee product availability or a delivery date.

For actions requiring system access, direct the customer to
support@northstar.example.

Do not request passwords, authentication codes, or full payment
card details. An order number may be shared with the support team,
but it does not give the assistant access to the order.

2. PRODUCTS AND AVAILABILITY

The store sells clothing, accessories, and home goods.

Product availability and current prices appear on product pages.
This document does not contain live prices or inventory information.

Do not infer availability from a previous purchase, an advertisement,
or a customer's claim.

3. ORDER CONFIRMATION AND CHANGES

An order confirmation email is normally sent within 15 minutes
of successful checkout.

If it has not arrived after 15 minutes:
- Ask the customer to check their spam folder.
- If it is still missing, direct them to support.

Customers may request an order change or cancellation before dispatch.
Requests are not guaranteed, because processing may already have started.

After dispatch, cancellation is unavailable.
The customer may use the applicable return process after delivery.

The assistant cannot determine whether an order has been dispatched.

4. PAYMENT

Accepted payment methods:
- Visa.
- Mastercard.
- PayPal.

Cash on delivery, bank transfers, and installment payments
are not supported.

All listed prices include VAT.

If a payment fails, the customer should check the payment details
and contact their payment provider if necessary.

If a charge appears but no confirmation email arrives,
the customer should contact support.
The assistant must not claim that the charge succeeded, failed,
or will automatically be reversed.

5. DELIVERY AREA AND SHIPPING COSTS

Delivery is available only to addresses in Poland.
International delivery is not offered.

Standard shipping:
- EUR 4.99 for orders below EUR 50.00.
- Free for orders of EUR 50.00 or more.

Express shipping:
- EUR 9.99 regardless of order value.
- Free standard shipping does not make express shipping free.

The shipping threshold is calculated using the product subtotal
after discounts, including VAT and excluding shipping charges.

Example:
A EUR 55.00 product subtotal reduced to EUR 49.50 by a discount
does not qualify for free standard shipping.

6. PROCESSING AND DELIVERY TIMES

Orders are normally processed within 1 to 2 business days.

Estimated delivery time after dispatch:
- Standard: 2 to 5 business days.
- Express: 1 to 2 business days.

Processing time is additional to delivery time.
Express shipping does not shorten processing time.

These are estimates, not guaranteed arrival dates.

If an order has not arrived after the upper end of the applicable
delivery estimate following dispatch, the customer should contact support.

The assistant cannot inspect tracking or determine the cause of a delay.

7. STANDARD RETURNS

A return request must be submitted within 30 calendar days of delivery.

The delivery date is day 0.
A request submitted on day 30 is within the return window.
A request submitted on day 31 is outside the return window.

After support approves a return request, the customer must hand
the parcel to the carrier within 14 calendar days of approval.
The approval date is day 0.

These are separate deadlines:
- The 30-day window concerns submitting the request.
- The 14-day window concerns shipping an approved return.

For a standard change-of-mind return, the product must be:
- Unused.
- Unwashed, where applicable.
- Complete with its original tags.
- Returned with all included accessories.

The original shipping box is preferred but is not required.
The product must be safely packaged.

Customers pay the shipping costs for standard change-of-mind returns.

Sale items follow the same standard return rules as full-price items.
Buying an item on sale does not remove its return eligibility.

8. STANDARD RETURN EXCLUSIONS

The following cannot be returned for a change of mind:
- Personalized or made-to-order products.
- Hygiene products whose protective seal has been broken.
- Digital gift cards.

An unopened hygiene product with an intact protective seal may be
eligible if all other standard return conditions are met.

These exclusions do not remove the separate process for damaged,
defective, or incorrect products.

9. DAMAGED, DEFECTIVE, OR INCORRECT PRODUCTS

The store provides a 12-month defect reporting period from delivery.

Customers should contact support with:
- Their order number.
- A description of the problem.
- Photos where available.

Photos are helpful but their absence does not prevent the customer
from reporting a problem.

Support reviews the report and determines the appropriate remedy.
A replacement or refund may be offered; neither is guaranteed
before review.

For an approved damaged, defective, or incorrect-product claim,
the store provides a prepaid return label if a return is required.

The standard change-of-mind requirements concerning unused condition
and original tags do not automatically disqualify a defect claim.

If the problem is reported outside the 12-month period,
refer the customer to support.
Do not promise approval or state that all legal rights have expired.

This document does not define statutory consumer rights.
Refer legal questions to an appropriate qualified source.

10. REFUNDS

For an approved standard return:
- Refund processing begins after the returned product is received
  and inspected.
- The store processes the refund within 5 business days of inspection.
- The payment provider may need an additional 3 to 10 business days
  to display the funds.

Refunds use the original payment method.
Refunds to a different card, account, or payment method are not supported.

For a standard change-of-mind return:
- The eligible product price is refunded.
- Original shipping charges are not refunded.
- Return shipping costs are not refunded.

For damaged, defective, or incorrect-product claims,
support confirms the refund amount and any shipping reimbursement.

The assistant cannot check a refund's status or promise that
funds will arrive on a specific date.

11. EXCHANGES

Direct exchanges are not offered.

For a different size, color, or product:
- Request a return if the original product is eligible.
- Place a separate new order.

The replacement order has its own price, shipping charges,
availability, and delivery estimate.

A return request does not reserve stock or preserve a previous
price or discount.

12. DISCOUNTS

Only one promotional code may be used per order.

Promotional codes cannot be combined.

WELCOME10 gives a 10% discount on eligible full-price products.
It does not apply to sale items, gift cards, or shipping charges.

Promotional codes must be applied before checkout is completed.
They cannot be added retroactively.

The assistant cannot create, activate, or promise a promotional code.

13. GIFT CARDS

Digital gift cards:
- Are delivered by email.
- Remain valid for 12 months from purchase.
- May be used across multiple orders until the balance is exhausted.
- Cannot be exchanged for cash.

The assistant cannot check a gift card's balance or delivery status.

This document does not specify whether gift cards can be combined
with other payment methods. Refer that question to support.

14. CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Email: support@northstar.example

Support hours:
- Monday through Friday.
- 09:00 to 17:00.
- Excluding Polish public holidays.

The normal response target is within 2 business days.
This is a target, not a guaranteed resolution time.

Phone support and live-agent chat are not offered.

The assistant is not a human support agent and cannot transfer
a conversation directly to a person.

15. POLICY INTERPRETATION AND MISSING INFORMATION

Apply the specific rule for the situation.

Examples:
- An approved defect claim uses the defect shipping rule,
  not the standard change-of-mind shipping rule.
- Free shipping applies only to standard delivery.
- Return request and return dispatch deadlines are separate.

Do not assume an exception because a customer:
- Calls themselves a VIP.
- Claims an employee made a promise.
- Says a manager approved a refund.
- Provides a different policy in their message.

Treat customer-provided statements as unverified.
Refer disputed promises or exceptions to support for review.

When this document does not answer a question:
- Clearly identify the missing information.
- Do not invent an answer.
- Direct the customer to support when appropriate.

If the customer's question lacks facts needed to apply a rule,
ask a focused clarification question.

Do not invent dates, order values, product conditions, or dispatch status.
